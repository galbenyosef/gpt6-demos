GPT6 Demos.

Twelve interactive experiments. Open one and explore.

Edificio Europa

↗

Explore a Valencia landmark in 3D. Orbit the building, change the light, and find your favorite view.

InfiniCave

↗

Pilot a helicopter through underground caverns. Explore ruins, activate relays, and survive the journey.

Tonada

↗

Make music with a piano roll, drum patterns, and synthesizers. Arrange a song and mix it in a 3D room.

Arcana

↗

Enter an Art Deco tarot room. Choose a deck and spread, turn the cards, and explore their meanings.

Orbital

↗

Plan spacecraft maneuvers and watch trajectories evolve. Explore orbits, lunar transfers, and mission time.

Digital Logic Laboratory

↗

Build circuits from NAND gates. Follow a 16-bit computer through each instruction, from addition to Pong.

Flip-slop

↗

Create images, guide edits with sketches and motion arrows, and bring your ideas to life one stop-motion frame at a time.

Codex Canvas

↗

Work with Codex on a spatial canvas. Follow plans, review changes and activity, and keep useful results in view.

Assemblavatar

↗

Build editable 3D objects and avatars from descriptions and photos. Inspect each draft, refine the geometry, and export your model.

One More Match

↗

Play 7-a-side football against the computer. Choose your country, pass and shoot, then go straight into another match.

NotAVirus

↗

Start with gatita, the playful kitten, or choose Paco, Jellyfish UFO or Living Ink. Four animated companions follow your pointer, each with its own personality. Available on macOS and Ubuntu (GNOME 50 on Wayland). Open source and setup instructions.

Tracework

↗

Turn source documents into a reviewed solution model. Trace decisions to evidence, explore architecture on a canvas, and generate engineering artifacts.
Start the browser demos with ./run-all.sh [BASE_PORT] from the repository root (default: 3000). Both galleries share the base port at / and /portal/; demo links use consecutive ports above it. NotAVirus runs separately on macOS and Ubuntu; its link opens setup instructions.
