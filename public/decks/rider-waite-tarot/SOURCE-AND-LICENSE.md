# Rider–Waite–Smith tarot — Pam-A scans

Downloaded 2026-09-05 from Wikimedia Commons.

## Contents

78 JPG card faces: 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana (14 each of Cups, Pentacles, Swords, and Wands). 41 full-resolution original scans and 37 Wikimedia-generated 960-pixel-wide versions are included. Wikimedia rate-limited original-file downloads, so its recommended thumbnail service supplied the remaining cards. Downloaded bytes are preserved. All 41 originals were checked against Commons SHA-1 checksums; every image was decoded and its dimensions verified.

Major Arcana filenames begin with RWS Tarot and use numbers 00–21. Minor Arcana filenames use Cups, Pents, Swords, or Wands followed by 01–14: Ace, Two through Ten, Page, Knight, Queen, King. This set contains card faces only.

## Source and public-domain status
