# NotAVirus product context

Native macOS accessory app, Rust + AppKit through objc2. Audience: desktop users who want a small animated character following the pointer without interrupting work. Success: launch, see the pet, retain normal clicks, switch/import packs, pause or quit from one menu.

Authority: specs/not-a-virus-spec.md revision 0.3 and IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md. A small transparent nonactivating panel is the entire visual surface; system menu and alert typography/chrome are intentional. No onboarding, preferences window, browser UI, custom dashboard, network, input injection, or TCC permissions.

The engine is complete independently of final art. Diagnostic numbered geometry is explicitly temporary. The supplied Paco/gatita JPEGs are reference art, not animation atlases. No finished character claim is allowed until T11 acceptance.
