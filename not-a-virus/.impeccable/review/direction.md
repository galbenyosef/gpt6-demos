# Direction contract — native NotAVirus

Mode: Operate for menu controls; Experience for the transparent sprite. Platform: macOS AppKit (not iOS/web).
Pinned brief: spec revision 0.3. Native status menu, no Dock icon, no preferences/onboarding shell. Use AppKit typography, spacing, focus, selection, disabled and checked states. Status mark ‽ is explicitly permitted by the spec. Art sizes are 128/192/256 points, clamped to screen bounds; transparent background, no shadow. Pack filtering and movement come from data.
First viewport: one small transparent sprite over the user's desktop. Signature interaction: bounded acceleration follows the pointer without intercepting clicks; held transitions and configurable personality are visible through numbered diagnostic frames.
Quality bar: upright top-left atlas sampling, stable feet/anchor, correct facing, stable idle/transition timing, pause freezes, one pack/one instance, honest diagnostic asset status. The numbered original rectangles are test fixtures mandated by the implementation plan, not a substitute for finished character art. No comp or aesthetic redesign is appropriate for these pinned native controls.
